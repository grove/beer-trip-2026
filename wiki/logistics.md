## Best overnight pattern

The default [dated itinerary](five-day-plan.md) proposes **Manchester nights Mon 9, Tue 10, Wed 11; Liverpool night Thu 12; Manchester night Fri 13**. It preserves a baggage-free Stockport day and avoids having to cross from Liverpool on your early departure morning. It involves two hotel moves and luggage storage; book accordingly. This is an editorial suggestion, not a reservation.

The less-mobile alternative is Manchester Mon/Tue and Liverpool Wed/Thu/Fri, then an **early, confirmed** direct train/transfer from Liverpool to Manchester Airport Saturday. For a **12:25 international departure**, build in a large buffer, because a missed/altered service has serious consequences. A single Manchester base with Liverpool day trips is also possible but sacrifices Liverpool evenings and increases train time. No hotels have been booked by this wiki.

## Airport ↔ Manchester rail and timing

Manchester Airport’s [train guide](https://www.manchesterairport.co.uk/getting-to-and-from/by-train/) says **about 20 minutes between the airport rail station and Manchester Piccadilly**, plus **5–15 minutes’ walk** between station and terminal, with service subject to the actual schedule.

The airport’s [check-in guidance](https://www.manchesterairport.co.uk/help/checking-in/) recommends being checked in and ready for security **at least two hours before departure**. Saturday **12:25** implies **10:25** as a minimum planning checkpoint; aim for the terminal around **10:00–10:15** (or earlier if bags/airline require), leaving central Manchester around **09:00 or earlier** with a verified train. Confirm the **terminal for SK4608** with the airline/airport when final details are published; no terminal is assumed.

On arrival Monday, do **not** equate the scheduled 17:50 touchdown with arrival at your Manchester hotel. Immigration, baggage, train, station walking and check-in can consume substantial time. In particular, [Marble Arch](venues/marble-arch.md) publishes **Monday last food orders at 20:00, bar last orders 22:00, Tuesday closed**: a drink is plausible, dinner there is tight. [Official opening and kitchen times](https://marblebeers.com/the-marble-arch/).

## Manchester, Liverpool and excursion rail

- **Manchester–Liverpool:** choose the correct departure station (Manchester Piccadilly, Oxford Road and Victoria are not interchangeable), and check the actual service to **Liverpool Lime Street**. The fastest publicised intercity service is not the basis for a rigid pub schedule. [National Rail: Liverpool by train](https://www.nationalrail.co.uk/discover-by-train/great-british-destinations/visit-liverpool-by-train/).
- **Stockport:** direct mainline connections from central Manchester; town pubs involve walks in different directions. [Petersgate](venues/petersgate-tap-stockport.md) and [Arden](venues/arden-arms-stockport.md) are old-town anchors; [Magnet](venues/magnet-stockport.md) is farther north and does **not open until about 16:00 Wednesday** according to its current listing.
- **Oldham:** accessible on the Bee Network tram, but **Fox & Pine** is a separate choice replacing another afternoon.
- **Rochdale, Stalybridge, Wigan, Macclesfield, St Helens, Chester and Wirral:** each is an *alternative* excursion, not an additive train pub crawl. Confirm whether the nearest station is really where your pub is, especially St Helens Central vs Junction, Warrington Central vs Bank Quay, and New Mills Central vs Newtown.
- **Wirral Festival at Hulme Hall, Bebington:** this is a separate venue from the Liverpool Ship & Mitre pub, with its own session times. Check the appropriate Merseyrail route and return before purchasing. [Festival](https://theshipandmitre.uk/event/ship-mitre-22nd-wirral-beer-festival/).

### Payment and local travel

In Greater Manchester, the [Bee Network contactless rules](https://tfgm.com/ways-to-pay/contactless) require bus **tap-on** and tram **tap-in/tap-out**, keeping the same card/device; each traveller should use their own. Transport capping does not mean every National Rail train is included.

Merseyrail [Day Savers](https://www.merseyrail.org/tickets-passes/daily-travel/day-saver/) have area/time limitations; the operator indicates Tap & Go is **not available at Chester**, so confirm your ticket before travelling there. The [network map](https://merseyrail.org/journey-planning/plan-your-journey/network-map/) is not the same as a live disruption report.

## A manageable way to drink and eat

Choose three or four **must-do** pub experiences per full day and let a fifth be optional. Order halves/thirds where offered (not every micropub offers halves), have meals and water, and avoid a full pint at every venue. Reserve only a couple of important food stops; do not assume a kitchen is still open because the bar is. Dress for cold, wet November walking and use daylight for rural excursions.
