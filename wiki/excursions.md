# Choosing the surrounding-area day

[Home](../README.md) · [Five-day plan](five-day-plan.md) · [Surrounding venues](areas/surroundings.md) · [Liverpool and Wirral](areas/liverpool.md)

The region has more worthwhile excursions than five days can hold. **Choose one main outing**, or replace a city day if a specialism matters enough. The time budgets below are editorial allowances for enjoying a cluster, including local travel; they are not scheduled train durations. Verify the actual route and return for your dates using the [logistics sources](logistics.md).

### Stockport: do the centre before expanding

Petersgate, Arden Arms and Swan with Two Necks provide a compact core with genuinely different experiences. Add the Magnet for cask breadth or [Runaway](venues/runaway-brewery-tap-stockport.md) for brewery/pizza interest on its opening days. The [Crown](venues/crown-inn-stockport.md) belongs to the viaduct side of the plan. [Olde Vic](venues/olde-vic-stockport.md), [Blossoms](venues/blossoms-stockport.md) and [Heaton Hops](venues/heaton-hops.md) earn their catalogue entries but should not all be squeezed into a short old-town walk. [Northumberland Arms at Marple Bridge](venues/northumberland-arms-marple-bridge.md) is a separate community-pub outing.

### Chester: give the cider specialist time

Start or finish with That Beer Place, allowing time for a guided choice from the actual cider/perry and beer selection. Brewery Tap supplies a particularly strong architectural and dining contrast. Old Harkers Arms and Cellar are useful towards the station/canal side; [Olde Cottage](venues/olde-cottage-chester.md) is another traditional station-side option. [Albion](venues/albion-chester.md) suits a traditional-atmosphere visit if its restricted hours fit. Do not substitute the Bear & Billet merely because an old guide calls it a classic beer pub: the [current exclusion note](changes-and-exclusions.md) explains why it is absent.

[Pied Bull](venues/pied-bull-chester.md) adds an actual advertised cellar-brewery tour and tasting, including an option marketed for two adults. Arrange a date directly; buying a voucher does not establish a November slot. This can replace one of the pub stops for a more structured production experience.

### Dunham: arrange the rural leg

The official Apple Barn page currently lists weekend opening, conflicting with older CAMRA hours. The orchard is not beside Altrincham Interchange; arrange the onward bus/taxi and return, and use daylight for the rural part. A public cider-bar opening is not a promised guided orchard or pressing tour. Altrincham's cask bars make a useful second part of the outing, with the important cider distinctions recorded on their pages.

### Rochdale, Wigan and Macclesfield: choose a pair

These work best as concentrated visits rather than as stops in a multi-town dash. Rochdale offers Baum plus Flying Horse; ask carefully about cider at the latter. Wigan Central plus Swan & Railway is a natural pairing, but check which of Wigan's two main stations your train uses. In Macclesfield, RedWillow plus Waters Green is an efficient core. [Cotton Tree, Bollington](venues/cotton-tree-bollington.md) is a worthwhile extension with cider and traditional atmosphere, requiring another transport leg. The closed Vale Inn should not be copied from older Bollington itineraries.

## Other Greater Manchester alternatives

**Chorlton** is the coherent suburban beer-and-cider cluster: [Beer House](venues/beer-house-chorlton.md), [Fell](venues/fell-chorlton.md) and [Chorlton Tap](venues/chorlton-tap.md). **Urmston** offers [Prairie Schooner](venues/prairie-schooner-urmston.md). **Eccles/Patricroft** is particularly interesting for the [Lamb](venues/lamb-hotel-eccles.md) and [Queens Arms](venues/queens-arms-patricroft.md), with the latter clearly marked as a heritage-only no-cask exception. These can replace a central Manchester block if you prefer neighbourhood pubs.

**Bury and Ramsbottom** can combine [Trackside](venues/trackside-bury.md) and [Irwell Works](venues/irwell-works-ramsbottom.md), but do not assume the heritage railway operates on your dates. Plan an ordinary public-transport alternative unless a dated railway service is confirmed. Trackside's real-cider badge conflicts with a newer branch report; read the note before making it a cider target. The [Bury festival](november-events.md) supplies a separate 6–7 November opportunity.

**Bolton and Horwich** suit a more focused local-brewery or traditional-pub interest: [Bank Top Brewery Tap](venues/bank-top-brewery-tap-bolton.md), [Olde Man & Scythe](venues/olde-man-scythe-bolton.md) and [Blackedge Brewery Bar](venues/blackedge-brewery-bar-horwich.md). They are not all in central Bolton, and the Olde Man & Scythe's historic cider reputation does not establish current real cider.

**Rivington** offers a distinct farm/reservoir setting at [Rivington Brewery Tap](venues/rivington-brewery-tap.md). It needs a rural outward-and-return plan and benefits from daylight; treat it as a dedicated excursion. **New Mills** offers the riverside mill setting and local beer of [Torr Vale Tap](venues/torr-vale-tap-new-mills.md). Choose the appropriate New Mills station and check the mill approach; do not assume Torrside's own seasonal brewery tap opens in November.

For a deliberately planned journey break, [Firkin in Newton-le-Willows](venues/firkin-newton-le-willows.md) and [Lower Angel in Warrington](venues/lower-angel-warrington.md) are worthwhile cask choices. Firkin has restricted opening, and not every Manchester–Liverpool service stops at Newton-le-Willows. Warrington Central and Bank Quay are different stations. These are optional stopping plans, not automatic additions to the intercity transfer.

## Farther east: reserve choices, not the default catchment

[Sportsman](venues/sportsman-huddersfield.md) and [Rat & Ratchet](venues/rat-ratchet-huddersfield.md) in Huddersfield, [Riverhead](venues/riverhead-marsden.md) in Marsden, and ['Udders Orchard](venues/udders-orchard-cidery.md) in Lindley are included for their specific beer/cider experiences. They are extended excursions beyond the main Manchester–Liverpool brief. Pick one coherent eastern day only if it replaces another plan.

'Udders advertises 7–8 November taproom openings on its current official page. That creates a real date-specific option for cider enthusiasts, but still requires the rail journey plus an onward Lindley connection. Do not assume a full railway pub trail is operational merely because its famous pubs remain open; check stops, engineering work and return travel for your particular dates.
