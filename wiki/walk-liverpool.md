# Liverpool beyond the pint — Thursday 12 and Friday 13 November

[Beyond the pint](walks-and-discoveries.md) · [Thursday and Friday itinerary](five-day-plan.md#thursday-12-november-arrive-liverpool-discover-dale-street) · [Liverpool pub stories](stories-liverpool.md) · [Manchester](walk-manchester.md)

**Two very different walks:** Thursday is the monumental station/civic quarter and Dale Street, possibly with an underground bunker or music; Friday is the Hope Street ridge, roofless church and two contrasting cathedrals. These follow the pub plan, not a generic all-city sightseeing trail. In November, take **outdoor views in daylight** and protect the Friday-evening return to Manchester if using the recommended hotel arrangement.

## Thursday 12 November: Lime Street to Dale Street

The planned pubs are [The Vines](venues/vines.md) → [Lion Tavern](venues/lion-tavern.md) → [Ship & Mitre](venues/ship-and-mitre.md), with one optional later pub **or** the Wirral beer festival. Don't add a full second tour before a festival transfer.

| Pub-route moment | Interesting stop | Time and evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Arriving at Lime Street, before The Vines** | **[St George's Hall](https://liverpoolcityhalls.co.uk/plan-your-visit/)**: the immense classical facade opposite the station is a photo opportunity even if you do not go in. It contains historic civic and court spaces, but **interior guided tours run on selected dates only**; the visitor centre is not a self-guided ticket to the whole hall. | **10–15 minutes outside.** Visitor centre is normally Mon–Sat 09:30–17:00; arrange a proper tour only if [the operator lists one](https://liverpoolcityhalls.co.uk/plan-your-visit/) for your date. |
| **Near St George's Hall, before heading toward the Lion** | **[Liverpool Central Library](https://liverpool.gov.uk/libraries/explore-central-library/central-library/)** on William Brown Street: step into the historic **Picton Reading Room** for a great contrast with the surrounding modern library. | **15–30 minutes.** Council lists **Thursday 09:00–20:00**, free public library; room/event access may vary. |
| **Between Dale Street venues, if you want music rather than another pub** | **[The Cavern Club](https://www.cavernclub.com/opening-times-and-prices/)**, Mathew Street: an easy, lively Beatles-associated detour with live music even during the day. It is a music venue serving drinks, but the reason to visit here is the **performance and atmosphere**, not another beer listing. | **30–45 minutes** minimum. Official site advertises daily music from **11:15**, Thursday venue hours **11:00–01:00** and **paid admission (£6 adult single entry)**; programme/prices may change. |
| **A substantial alternative west of the Lion/Dale Street cluster** | **[Western Approaches HQ](https://liverpoolwarmuseum.co.uk/visiting/information/)**, Rumford Street: go underground into the Second World War command bunker used in the Battle of the Atlantic. Old telephones, plotting rooms and working spaces make a more hands-on, cinematic experience. | **About 75 minutes inside**, per [the museum](https://liverpoolwarmuseum.co.uk/visiting/explore/); it lists **Thursday 10:00–18:00, last entry 17:00**, ticketed. **Replace one or two pubs** rather than squeezing it between all of them. |
| **If skipping extra pubs and the festival** | Walk west to **[Pier Head and the Three Graces](https://www.visitliverpool.com/explore/liverpool-city-centre/waterfront/)**: the Liver Building, river, ferry frontage and city skyline are a powerful introduction to the port. You can continue to Albert Dock, but that becomes an extended walk away from Dale Street. | **20–45 minutes for an outdoor waterfront stroll, plus the extra walk each way.** The [Royal Liver Building tour](https://liverbuildingtour.com/the-experience/) is a separate timed/ticketed experience, not an opportunistic short stop. |

**Thursday trade-off:** if attending the [Wirral Beer Festival](november-events.md) at Hulme Hall from 17:00, prioritise the **St George's Hall exterior and/or Picton Reading Room** before the core three pubs, then leave time for the train and onward walk. Choose the bunker or waterfront **instead of** the extra pubs or festival, not in addition.

## Friday 13 November: Hope Street and the cathedral corridor

The core pub sequence is [Roscoe Head](venues/roscoe-head.md) → [Philharmonic Dining Rooms](venues/philharmonic-dining-rooms.md) → [Peter Kavanagh's](venues/peter-kavanaghs.md) → perhaps [Ye Cracke](venues/ye-cracke.md) or [Belvedere](venues/belvedere.md). This area is particularly good for **non-beer architecture within a short corridor**.

| Where to weave it in | Discovery | Suggested commitment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **From Roscoe Head toward Hope Street** | **[St Luke's Bombed Out Church](https://www.slboc.com/the-story)**, Leece Street: roofless remains of a church destroyed in the 1941 May Blitz, now a memorial and arts venue. Looking at the shell from the street is worthwhile even if the grounds are shut. | **10–20 minutes**; [the operator](https://www.slboc.com/) lists winter **10:00–16:00** on its normal Wednesday–Sunday pattern, **subject to weather, events and individual open days**. |
| **Toward the northern end of Hope Street, before the Philharmonic** | **[Liverpool Metropolitan Cathedral](https://liverpoolmetrocathedral.org.uk/visit/)**: a startling modern circular building and lantern of coloured light. It's a beautiful opposite to the Anglican cathedral farther south. | **15–30 minutes.** Normally **daily 07:30–18:00**, free cathedral entry, with worship/event limitations. |
| **If you want a hidden architectural space** | **[Lutyens' Crypt](https://liverpoolmetrocathedral.org.uk/visit/the-lutyens-crypt/)** beneath the Metropolitan Cathedral: huge brick vaulting surviving from an abandoned grand-cathedral scheme. | **30–45 minutes extra**; official Friday listing **10:00–16:00 (last entry 15:30)**, **£5 adult**, ticketed, so trade time with a pub. |
| **After the Philharmonic, near Peter Kavanagh's** | **[Liverpool Cathedral](https://liverpoolcathedral.org.uk/plan-your-visit/opening-hours/)**: enter the immense Gothic space and experience the dramatic difference from the Metropolitan Cathedral. The route south along Hope Street joins the two. | **20–40 minutes** for the main cathedral, normally **Friday 10:00–18:00**, usually free. The optional [tower viewpoint](https://liverpoolcathedral.org.uk/plan-your-visit/the-cathedral-tower-experience/) is ticketed and involves **108 stairs after lifts**; weather and bell-ringing can alter access. |
| **Rather than several afternoon pubs** | **[Williamson Tunnels Heritage Centre](https://williamsontunnels.uk/)**, Smithdown Lane: eccentric subterranean brick-and-stone tunnels associated with Joseph Williamson. This is farther east than the natural Hope Street line, so make it a **booked special experience**, not a casual detour. | **Book ahead**: the Society lists tours **Friday–Sunday** at scheduled times from **10:30 to 15:30**, with **telephone reservation required**. Alternative [Friends of Williamson's Tunnels](https://williamsontunnels.com/visit-the-tunnels/) tours are **Wednesday/Sunday**, **not Friday**. Pick the correct organisation and entrance. |

**Friday trade-off:** for an easy, varied walk, do **Bombed Out Church exterior → Metropolitan Cathedral → Philharmonic → Anglican Cathedral → Peter Kavanagh's**, while retaining Roscoe Head and perhaps **one** final pub. Add the crypt or tunnels **only by removing more pub time**, and leave time to return to Manchester. If you instead choose the [Friday Wirral festival](november-events.md), that replaces almost the entire city walk.

## Useful distinctions

- **Free quick looks:** St George's Hall exterior, Central Library, waterfront, the two cathedrals' main spaces, Bombed Out Church from the street.
- **Ticketed or schedule-dependent:** Cavern performances, Western Approaches, Royal Liver Building tower, St George's Hall guided interiors, Lutyens' Crypt, cathedral tower and Williamson Tunnels. No reservation has been made.
- **Don't plan outdated museum stops:** the [Maritime Museum is currently closed for waterfront redevelopment](https://www.visitliverpool.com/things-to-do-in-liverpool/attractions-in-liverpool/museums-and-galleries/); don't count on it as a walk-in backup.
