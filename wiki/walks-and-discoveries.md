**9–14 November 2026: Manchester, Stockport and Liverpool, on foot.** A companion to the beer and cider guide, not a second itinerary to complete. These are **unusual buildings, tunnels, independent streets, music and city stories** that sit close to the existing pub anchors. The pub days still drive the route; sightseeing fills the gaps.

**How to choose:** try **one or two quick discoveries per day** (roughly 10–30 minutes each), or trade **one pub** for a longer museum, tour or performance. The longer options are alternatives, **not additional compulsory stops**. Times suggested for visits are editorial allowances, not measured walks, bookings or guaranteed entry. Street routes are indicative; use walking directions for actual turn-by-turn navigation and allow for hills, crossings, weather and luggage.

## The simple rule

- **On the way:** an exterior, unusual street or free building that adds very little detour.
- **Choose a pause:** 20–45 minutes in a museum, art space, church or music venue. It may mean a shorter pub visit.
- **Replace a pub:** a paid tour or a substantial experience of 60–120 minutes. Keep only the pubs you most want, with enough time for a proper meal.

**Dates matter.** Do not recommend an attraction merely because it is famous: the [John Rylands Library](https://www.library.manchester.ac.uk/rylands/visit/) and [People's History Museum](https://phm.org.uk/visit/) are regularly **closed Tuesday**; [Hat Works](https://www.stockport.gov.uk/landing/hat-works-museum) and the [Stockport Market Hall](https://www.stockport.gov.uk/stockport-market/market-opening-times) are regularly **closed Wednesday**. These are fine for another visit, but **not indoor stops on the currently allocated days**.

**November reality:** daylight is short; choose outdoor views earlier, not after the last pint. Opening patterns and ticket costs linked in the city pages were checked **29 October 2026** and may change by 9–13 November. Check each operator's date-specific notices, particularly for tours, worship, special events and the weather-sensitive Bombed Out Church. No tickets or visits have been booked. The [interactive pub map](https://grove.github.io/beer-trip-2026/map/) plots **pubs**, not these new attraction entrances; use the official attraction addresses and live pedestrian directions separately.
