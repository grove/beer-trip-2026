# Shortlists for this trip

[Home](../README.md) · **[All venues ranked 1–158](rankings.md)** · [Dated flight-aware plan](five-day-plan.md) · [Complete catalogue](catalogue.md)

These are editorial cuts for two experienced beer travellers. Each linked venue page contains the evidence, address, food information and qualifications. Start here, then use the larger directories to substitute according to opening days and interests. An entry can belong to several shortlists because the best stops combine beer, atmosphere and a reason to linger.

## New 2026 award winners and substantial beer/cider discoveries

**[Fox & Pine, Oldham](venues/fox-and-pine-oldham.md)** adds a ten-cask, dark-beer and five/six real-cider/perry destination recognised by the Rochdale/Oldham/Bury branch in 2026. **[Our Gracie’s, Rochdale](venues/our-gracies-rochdale.md)** won that branch's 2026 cider award, with eight reported cider/perry choices. **[The Coop, Crosby](venues/coop-crosby.md)** is Liverpool branch Pub of the Year 2025 and 2026, with cask and real cider. These three make excellent *alternatives* to the default Stockport day, not compulsory additions.

**[Café Beermoth](venues/cafe-beermoth.md)** and **[Marble Arch](venues/marble-arch.md)** hold Central Manchester’s 2026 pub and cider awards respectively. **[Turks Head](venues/turks-head-st-helens.md)** is a national 2026 pub finalist and branch cider winner. Around Bolton/Horwich, **[B33R@33](venues/b33r-at-33-horwich.md)** and **[Bank Top Brewery Ale House](venues/bank-top-ale-house-horwich.md)** hold 2026 cider/pub awards; **[Broad Street Social](venues/broad-street-social-bury.md)** is Bury's branch winner. The new Manchester urban choices include **[Fell Northern Quarter](venues/fell-northern-quarter.md)** (cask/cider) and **[Pigeon Beer Wanderer](venues/pigeon-beer-wanderer.md)** (newer brewery-arch bar).

The **[global ranking](rankings.md)** places 158 venues in a complete order, while the **[9–14 November schedule](five-day-plan.md)** applies the four-full-day feasibility constraint. *Marble is officially closed Tuesday*: Monday arrival evening or Wednesday lunchtime is better.

## Spectacular and traditional interiors

For maximum visual impact, prioritise **Marble Arch, Crown & Kettle, Mr Thomas’s, Philharmonic, Peter Kavanagh’s and The Vines** from the tables above. For smaller surviving pub spaces, prioritise **Roscoe Head, Lion Tavern and Peveril**.

Outside the two centres, the strongest compact combination is [Arden Arms](venues/arden-arms-stockport.md) plus [Swan with Two Necks](venues/swan-with-two-necks-stockport.md) in Stockport. [Stalybridge Buffet Bar](venues/stalybridge-buffet-bar.md) adds a railway setting. [Swan & Railway](venues/swan-railway-wigan.md) can partner Wigan Central. [Lamb Hotel](venues/lamb-hotel-eccles.md) and [Queens Arms, Patricroft](venues/queens-arms-patricroft.md) form a specialist Eccles-area interest; Queens Arms is explicitly a heritage exception with no current cask reported.

On the Wirral, [Stork Hotel](venues/stork-hotel-birkenhead.md) is a spectacular **heritage-only exception with no cask documented**; pair it with [Gallaghers](venues/gallaghers-birkenhead.md) for the ale part of a Birkenhead visit. [Wheatsheaf at Raby](venues/wheatsheaf-raby.md) offers a remarkable snug but needs rural transport and care over which handpump beer is actually cask. [Scotch Piper](venues/scotch-piper.md) is a country-pub option north of Liverpool; its page qualifies the often-repeated age claim.

## Brewery and fermentation experiences

If time is limited, **[Balance](venues/balance-brewing-blending.md)** adds the most distinctive wild-beer/cider specialism. For a Piccadilly brewery circuit, choose among [Track](venues/track-brewery-tap.md), [Cloudwater](venues/cloudwater-unit-9.md), [Sureshot](venues/sureshot-taproom.md) and [Steelfish](venues/steelfish-taproom.md), checking each venue's cask evidence and opening. Visiting all of them would use time otherwise spent in traditional pubs.

In Liverpool, [Black Lodge](venues/black-lodge-liverpool.md) offers brewery interest with cask evidence, while [Azvex](venues/azvex-taproom.md) is a modern brewery specialist. [Glen Affric](venues/glen-affric-taproom.md) can be an addition to Birkenhead. A taproom visit does not imply a guided tour or access to production space; arrange any advertised tour or tasting through the operator.

[Runaway's current Stockport taproom](venues/runaway-brewery-tap-stockport.md) adds cask, documented real cider and pizza to a Stockport visit. Use its Astley Street address rather than old Manchester brewery directions.

For a bookable production experience, [Pied Bull in Chester](venues/pied-bull-chester.md) advertises a cellar tour and tasting suitable for two; confirm the date. [Rivington](venues/rivington-brewery-tap.md) is the scenic farm-taproom alternative, while [Torr Vale Tap](venues/torr-vale-tap-new-mills.md) adds a riverside industrial setting and local/Torrside beer. Their transport and opening constraints matter to the value of the detour.
