## Pick one proper experience

### Staircase House: a peculiar historic house and rare staircase

The [15th-century Staircase House](https://www.stockport.gov.uk/landing/staircase-house), 30–31 Market Place, is literally in the pub quarter. The entertaining hook is a **rare Jacobean cage-newel staircase**, hands-on history and an audio guide. The council currently lists **Wednesday 10:00–17:00**, last admission **one hour 15 minutes before closing**; [adult admission £6](https://www.stockport.gov.uk/planning-your-visit-to-staircase-house). [What makes the house unusual](https://www.stockport.gov.uk/about-staircase-house).

**Best fit:** **before lunch** if you reach the centre early enough, then Arden's Wednesday lunch, Swan and Petersgate. Budget **45–75 minutes inside**; this is a planning allowance, not a published tour duration. Choose this if interiors, historical oddities and hands-on displays appeal.

### Air Raid Shelters: wander through tunnels beneath the town

[Stockport Air Raid Shelters](https://www.stockport.gov.uk/landing/air-raid-shelters), **65 Chestergate**, are cut into sandstone. The old 'Chestergate Hotel' nickname and recreated wartime conditions make this much more immersive than another conventional local museum. The [council says Wednesday 13:00–17:00, last admission 16:00](https://www.stockport.gov.uk/planning-your-visit-to-the-air-raid-shelters/air-raid-shelters-opening-hours), with [adult self-guided admission £6](https://www.stockport.gov.uk/tours-air-raid-shelters). Evening explorer tours are **separately scheduled and booked**; don't infer one for 11 November.

**Best fit:** take an early Arden lunch, visit the shelters **after 13:00**, then choose which of Swan/Petersgate/Magnet to keep. Allow **45–75 minutes** as an editorial visit budget. Do not assume it can slot into the existing four-pub timetable without shortening something.

## What *not* to plan for this Wednesday

- [Hat Works](https://www.stockport.gov.uk/landing/hat-works-museum) is a tempting industrial-history museum by the station, but the **council lists Wednesday closed**, Thursday–Saturday **10:00–16:00**. Other old local listings show different hours; the council's current details take precedence.
- [Stockport Market Hall](https://www.stockport.gov.uk/stockport-market/market-opening-times) is worth appreciating **from the outside**. The ordinary indoor market trading days exclude Wednesday.
- Trying **both museums plus all four core pubs** makes a crowded day; choose **one**, or consciously trade two pubs for an extended history outing.

**Practical:** the old town has steep and sometimes cobbled approaches, and the Magnet is north of the main cluster.
