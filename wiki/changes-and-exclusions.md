## Opening and kitchen conflicts

- **[Marble Arch](venues/marble-arch.md):** official Tuesday closure conflicts with CAMRA's schedule. The official page itself also retains general kitchen wording; confirm directly rather than constructing a definitive Tuesday timetable. [Official page](https://marblebeers.com/the-marble-arch/).
- **[Dunham Apple Barn](venues/dunham-apple-barn.md):** the current producer page's weekend-only opening takes priority over older weekday listings, subject to direct reconfirmation.
- **[Track](venues/track-brewery-tap.md), [Sureshot](venues/sureshot-taproom.md), [Black Lodge Liverpool](venues/black-lodge-liverpool.md):** published days/times differ across sources. Check the actual date, especially early-week or winter Sunday visits.
- **[Ship & Mitre](venues/ship-and-mitre.md) and [Pen Factory](venues/pen-factory.md):** kitchen schedules differ between pub and guide. A pub being open does not establish that a meal is available.
- **[Cloudwater tours](venues/cloudwater-unit-9.md):** use the current booking calendar; a static tour description or taproom opening time does not establish a November slot.
- **Manchester Cider Club at [Balance](venues/balance-brewing-blending.md):** CAMRA pages disagree on the recurring weekday. Require a dated organiser listing rather than calculating a November meeting.

## Seasonal and experience-only leads

**Blackjack Brewery Tap** is not included as a guaranteed November opening. The researched [Central Manchester CAMRA pub guide](https://centralmanchester.camra.org.uk/pubguide) advertised summer 2026 weekends; [Smithfield Market Tavern](venues/smithfield-market-tavern.md) supplies a useful Blackjack pub experience unless a November brewery event is announced.

**Torrside's New Mills brewery tap** is also a seasonal lead rather than a confirmed November opening. The [official opening page](https://www.torrside.co.uk/opening-times.html) describes warmer-month bar weekends and separate Christmas openings; weekday bottle-shop access is takeaway only. No November bar date was established. [Torr Vale Tap](venues/torr-vale-tap-new-mills.md) is the researched local draught alternative. This is not a claim that Torrside has closed.

**Robinsons' former Stockport brewery tours** should not appear as a current bookable experience. The [official visitor page](https://www.robinsonsbrewery.com/visitors-centre-and-shires/) says tours ended in May 2024 and brewing no longer takes place at the central Old Unicorn site. A small museum/shop remains a possible weekday heritage stop, subject to current opening; it does not establish a tour or tasting. [Pied Bull, Chester](venues/pied-bull-chester.md) offers a separately researched cellar-tour option.

**Baltic Fleet** is a current cask-pub recommendation, but this wiki does not promise an operating downstairs Wapping brewery or a production tour. **Scotch Piper** is recommended for its surviving country-pub character without repeating its promotional 1320 origin as established physical dating. The individual venue pages preserve both distinctions.

The [November event guide](november-events.md) records confirmed advertised dates and ticket caveats. Announced events are separate from ordinary pub opening, and available web evidence is not a booking confirmation.
