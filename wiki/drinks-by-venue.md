**Research checked 28 October 2026.** These are *documented possibilities*, not predictions or live lists for **9–13 November 2026**. The guide covers **all 158 longlisted venues**, including alternative excursions that cannot fit the trip. Use the links to revisit each underlying CAMRA/official source close to departure.

**Read the labels:** *Regular* means the operator/CAMRA lists a repeat offering, not an availability guarantee. *Recently recorded* is historical and may already have changed. *Changing* means only the brewer is a useful clue. Cask and brewery-conditioned keg are not interchangeable. Likewise, a cider brand, a box, a handpump or a clouded drink is **not** proof of CAMRA-qualifying real cider: check the specific product and fresh-pressed juice criteria ([CAMRA FAQ](https://camra.org.uk/promotional-campaigns/promoting-real-cider-and-perry/faqs)).

## Short drink leads for the actual route

- **Monday Manchester (if the pub is reachable):** Marble Arch — Marble Pint or Manchester Bitter, plus a changing real cider. The operator's Tuesday closure makes Monday / Wednesday more useful.
- **Tuesday Manchester:** City Arms — Brightside Odin Blonde; Peveril — Millstone Tiger Rut, Titanic Plum Porter or Timothy Taylor Landlord; Gas Lamp — changing Pomona Island cask and Little Pomona real cider.
- **Wednesday Stockport:** Arden Arms / Swan with Two Necks — Robinsons Unicorn or Dizzy Blonde; Old Tom is seasonal after its 7 Nov launch, not guaranteed; Petersgate Tap — a changing Vocation/RedWillow cask and cellar real cider or perry.
- **Thursday Liverpool:** Lion Tavern — Otter Bitter regular plus guests; Ship & Mitre — changing Black Lodge/Colbier and real cider; Vines — Ossett Silver King or Titanic Plum Porter as named regulars.
- **Friday Liverpool:** Roscoe Head — Tetley Bitter, Timothy Taylor Landlord or Fyne Ales Jarl if still trading; Peter Kavanagh's — Greene King Abbot or Landlord; then the changing cask board at Philharmonic.

## Ordering checklist

Ask which exact **beer and producer** is currently pouring, whether it is really **cask-conditioned** rather than brewery-conditioned beer through a handpump, and whether a given named **cider/perry product** qualifies as fresh-pressed under current CAMRA rules. Different bottles from the same producer can differ. Recent sightings and awards guide discovery but are not stock guarantees. For all venues check the [published opening-hours caveats](opening-hours.md) before planning travel.
