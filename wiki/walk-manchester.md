**The brief:** make the walk around [Mr Thomas's Chop House](venues/mr-thomass-chop-house.md), [City Arms](venues/city-arms.md), [Peveril of the Peak](venues/peveril-of-the-peak.md), [Britons Protection](venues/britons-protection.md) and [Gas Lamp](venues/gas-lamp.md) more memorable. **Free art and curious architecture** are the easy wins; the one highly time-specific museum goes before lunch or replaces a pub. No need to tack on a separate half-day excursion.

## Worth changing the timetable for: a Victorian police station

**[Greater Manchester Police Museum](https://gmpmuseum.co.uk/visiting/opening-hours/), 57A Newton Street, M1 1ET.** An unexpectedly fun and atmospheric former police-station museum, with cells, equipment and local crime/policing history in the Northern Quarter. It is **open to the public on Tuesdays only**, normally **10:30–16:00 with last admission 15:00**, free entry, so it happens to fit *your* Tuesday exceptionally well.

**How to fit it:** visit **around 10:30–11:30 before lunch** and walk back toward the central pub cluster; alternatively replace a mid-afternoon pub. Do **not** leave it until the Fell/Café Beermoth evening choice, when it will normally be closed. [Official visit details](https://gmpmuseum.co.uk/visiting/opening-hours/) · [Official address](https://gmpmuseum.co.uk/about/contact/).

## Three sensible versions

1. **Mostly pubs:** Central Library → Mr Thomas's lunch → City Arms → Peveril → Britons Protection → Gas Lamp. The library and exterior observations add atmosphere without consuming the beer day.
2. **Art-and-ale:** Central Library → Manchester Art Gallery → lunch → **three or four** of the core pubs. Don't append a second museum.
3. **Curiosities day:** Police Museum before lunch → central pubs → Northern Quarter street-art wander later; skip Castlefield.

**The tempting Tuesday closures:** the magnificent [John Rylands Library](https://www.library.manchester.ac.uk/rylands/visit/) is usually **open Wednesday–Saturday, 10:00–17:00** and therefore **closed Tuesday**. The [People's History Museum](https://phm.org.uk/visit/) is also normally **closed Tuesday**. The National Trust's [Castlefield Viaduct](https://www.nationaltrust.org.uk/visit/cheshire-greater-manchester/castlefield-viaduct) normally opens Wednesday–Sunday. You can admire outdoor exteriors or revisit on another Manchester day if your schedule changes; don't allocate Tuesday ticket/entry time to them.
