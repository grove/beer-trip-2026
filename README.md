# Manchester & Liverpool beer hunt · 9–14 November 2026

A source-backed, LLM-friendly wiki for two experienced beer and cider enthusiasts flying **Oslo → Manchester on Monday 9 November** and **Manchester → Oslo on Saturday 14 November**. This edition prioritises **cask/real ale, qualifying real cider and perry, traditional and spectacular pubs, exceptional beer experiences and worthwhile food**. It now has **158 individual venue profiles** with original summaries and dated links to CAMRA, operators, breweries and heritage information.

**Read first:** The flights create **four full days, Tuesday 10–Friday 13 November, plus a Monday arrival evening**, not five full sightseeing days. The Sunday/late-month festival suggestions from the initial edition are no longer relevant. The proposed plan keeps **Friday night in Manchester** ahead of Saturday’s **12:25** international departure. All planning times are local UK time.

## Recommended balance

**Mon 9:** arrive Manchester **17:50**, allow for airport transfer, hope for **[Marble Arch](wiki/venues/marble-arch.md)** in the evening; its **Monday kitchen last order 20:00** is tight and **Tuesday is closed**. Protect Wednesday lunchtime as a backup.

**Tue 10:** Manchester’s historic rooms, ale and cider: [City Arms](wiki/venues/city-arms.md), [Mr Thomas’s](wiki/venues/mr-thomass-chop-house.md), [Peveril of the Peak](wiki/venues/peveril-of-the-peak.md), [Britons Protection](wiki/venues/britons-protection.md) and [Gas Lamp](wiki/venues/gas-lamp.md). Choose an optional evening destination, not another compulsory crawl.

**Wed 11:** **Stockport** is the best *combined* day of old rooms, cask and cider: [Arden Arms](wiki/venues/arden-arms-stockport.md), [Swan with Two Necks](wiki/venues/swan-with-two-necks-stockport.md), [Petersgate Tap](wiki/venues/petersgate-tap-stockport.md), [Magnet](wiki/venues/magnet-stockport.md). **Oldham’s Fox & Pine** is an outstanding alternative for ale and real cider, with a verified 2026 branch award.

**Thu 12:** arrive **Liverpool**; [The Vines](wiki/venues/vines.md), [Lion Tavern](wiki/venues/lion-tavern.md), [Ship & Mitre](wiki/venues/ship-and-mitre.md). You may swap the late afternoon for the **[Ship & Mitre 22nd Wirral Beer Festival](wiki/november-events.md)** opening evening.

**Fri 13:** Liverpool’s [Roscoe Head](wiki/venues/roscoe-head.md), [Philharmonic](wiki/venues/philharmonic-dining-rooms.md), [Peter Kavanagh’s](wiki/venues/peter-kavanaghs.md) and one further old pub. Alternatively choose the festival’s quiet **10:00–16:00** session instead of the whole pub walk. Train back to Manchester in the evening if using the recommended hotel pattern.

**Sat 14:** **Manchester Airport**, departure **12:25**. Aim for the terminal around **10:00–10:15** (earlier if necessary), not a morning pub stop.

This is a planned route, not a guarantee that a listed pint, food service, festival ticket or train will be available on the day.

The expanded edition links **269 distinct venue-source URLs** through **277 venue evidence uses**. **77 venue records** have documented real-cider evidence, **including explicit occasional supply**, so this does not mean 77 guaranteed daily cider ranges. The 2026 updates added Fox & Pine, The Coop, Our Gracie’s, Fell Northern Quarter and other strong candidates, plus current awards for Café Beermoth, Marble Arch and more.

**Opening-hours coverage (researched 28 October 2026):** all **158 venues** now have a separate opening-hours record: **157 normal weekly schedules** and **one event-only producer**. Each venue page cites its operator or CAMRA hours source, displays after-midnight closings explicitly and lists kitchen times separately when documented. These are ordinary *published* schedules, not confirmed trading on your dates. The [venue finder](https://grove.github.io/beer-trip-2026/explore/) can filter by a selected day’s published opening; the [map](https://grove.github.io/beer-trip-2026/map/) shows published hours for its selected trip day. Check critical anchors directly before travel.

Every venue is included for a specific reason. Heritage-only stops without cask and modern-beer specialists are clearly identified as exceptions. The global ranking is an **editorial comparative judgement, not firsthand tasting results**. Each page separates cider evidence from cask and explains opening/food limitations. The complete longlist is for **choice and flexibility**, not for attempting to drink at 158 places.

## LLM and maintenance use

Start with [llms.txt](llms.txt), the [opening-hours data](data/opening-hours.json), the [wiki index](wiki/index.md), the [ranking JSON](data/rankings.json), the [canonical venue JSON](data/venues.json) and the [events JSON](data/events.json). IDs are stable across prose pages and structured records; the [source registry](data/sources.json) records what each URL supports.

Python **3.9+** and no third-party packages:

```bash
python3 scripts/build_wiki.py
python3 scripts/build_rankings.py
python3 scripts/export_maps.py
python3 scripts/build_wiki.py --check
python3 scripts/build_rankings.py --check
python3 scripts/export_maps.py --check
python3 scripts/validate_wiki.py
```

The checks establish file/data consistency, not live opening, ticket, beer/cider condition or public transport. To change a recommendation, edit the canonical JSON and rebuild; update the flight-aware planning pages and [maintenance log](wiki/log.md) by hand. See [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md). The original repository Apache licence remains untouched.
